Our Story

Where luxury meets affordability for your beloved pets

Welcome to Velvet Paws LLC

We believe every pet deserves premium comfort, quality, and care — without the premium price tag. At Velvet Paws, we've reimagined pet retail by combining luxury aesthetics with dropship efficiency, bringing you curated pet supplies that your furry friends will love and your wallet will appreciate.

Our Philosophy

Traditional pet stores operate with hefty markups that make premium products inaccessible to many pet parents. We saw this problem and created a solution: partner directly with trusted suppliers in the US and EU to eliminate unnecessary middlemen, warehousing costs, and retail overhead.

The result? You get the same high-quality products at 40-60% less than traditional retail prices. Your pets get the comfort they deserve. And we get to help more pet parents spoil their companions without breaking the bank.

What Makes Us Different

Curated Selection: We don't sell everything. Every product in our catalog is hand-selected based on quality, customer reviews, and our own testing. If we wouldn't buy it for our own pets, we won't sell it to you.

Transparent Pricing: You'll notice our "compare at" prices aren't inflated fiction — they're what you'd actually pay at traditional retailers. Our prices are better because our model is smarter.

Fast, Reliable Fulfillment: By partnering with US and EU-based suppliers, we deliver premium products with shipping speeds that rival Amazon. Most orders ship within 1-3 business days.

Quality You Can Trust: We work exclusively with suppliers who maintain strict quality standards. Every product is inspected before it ships. If something arrives damaged or doesn't meet expectations, we make it right — no questions asked.

Our Commitment to You

Your pet's happiness is our mission. We're not just selling products — we're helping you provide the best life possible for your four-legged (or winged, or scaled) family members. Whether you have a playful puppy, a senior cat, a curious hamster, or a chatty parrot, we're here to support you with premium supplies at prices that make sense.

The Velvet Paws Promise

  • Premium quality at dropship prices
  • Curated products we'd use for our own pets
  • Fast shipping from US/EU suppliers
  • Hassle-free returns and exchanges
  • Real customer support from real pet lovers

Thank you for trusting us with your pet's comfort and happiness. We're honored to be part of your journey together.
