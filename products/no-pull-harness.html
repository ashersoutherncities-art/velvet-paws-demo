Say goodbye to pulling and hello to enjoyable walks. Our No-Pull Harness features front and back clip options that gently discourage pulling without choking or discomfort. Padded chest plate and reflective stitching for safe nighttime walks.

Features:

  • Front + back D-ring clip options
  • Padded, breathable mesh chest panel
  • 4 adjustable straps for perfect fit
  • Reflective stitching for night visibility
  • Sizes: XS to XL (see size chart)

How It Works:

When your dog pulls forward, the front-clip design gently redirects them back toward you, discouraging the pulling behavior. Unlike traditional collars that can damage the throat and trachea, this harness distributes pressure across the chest and shoulders.

Sizing Guide:

  • XS: Chest 13-16" (Chihuahua, Toy Poodle)
  • S: Chest 16-20" (Beagle, Corgi, Dachshund)
  • M: Chest 20-26" (Cocker Spaniel, Border Collie)
  • L: Chest 26-32" (Labrador, Golden Retriever)
  • XL: Chest 32-40" (German Shepherd, Rottweiler)

Measure around the widest part of your dog's chest, just behind the front legs.
